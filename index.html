How it works

One short path from a problem on the ground to a closed report.

ReportChoose the type, add the location and describe the problem.
PendingThe report is saved with a date and shows as Pending.
ActionThe responsible person inspects the spot and fixes the problem.
ResolvedThe report is marked Resolved, or reopened if the problem returns.
